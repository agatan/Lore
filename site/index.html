Life Log

What you said,
next to your calendar.

Meetings, calls, thinking out loud. Lore keeps it as time-stamped text on one Timeline with your calendar events, so you can see what was said and when.

  • Start it yourself, or on a schedule.Pick days and hours, like weekdays 9 to 6, and Lore records only then.
  • Speech in, silence out.Background noise and silence are skipped. Only actual speech is transcribed, on your Mac.
  • Your day at a glance.Time spoken, counts and events line up on one axis. Click any point to jump there.

Mini Window

A small Timeline that stays in front, even over full-screen apps, with a field for quick memos.

Search

Search speech, memos and dictations at once with ⌘F. Arrow to a result and jump straight to that moment.

Voice Memo

Press a shortcut and say it. It's saved as a memo on today's Timeline, exactly as you said it.

Dictation

Speak,
and it's typed.

Press a hotkey, talk, and Lore types formatted text right where your cursor is, in whatever app you're using.

  • Tap to toggle, hold to talk.Right Option by default. Works over any app; Esc cancels.
  • Modes for each app.Lore picks a Mode from the app you're in, so chat and email can get different instructions. Your Vocabulary applies to every Mode.
  • Formatting stays on your Mac.Apple Foundation Models by default, with Ollama and LM Studio as local options. Cloud models only if you choose one.
  • You never lose the words.If formatting fails, you get the raw transcript. If the text can't be inserted, it's left on your clipboard.

Your AI

Bring
your own AI.

Lore records. It doesn't write summaries or to-do lists. When you want a recap or a clean draft, hand your Life Log to the AI you already use.

  • CLI.lore exports a day as Markdown, searches everything, and returns date ranges as JSON for scripts.
  • MCP.Add lore-mcp to your AI client, and it can read any day or range of days.
  • Read-only and local.Both run on your Mac over stdio, never change your data, and Lore makes no network connection for them.
# Copy today as Markdown
$ lore export day --clipboard

# Search past conversations
$ lore search "release plan" --limit 20

# A week of speech and memos, as JSON
$ lore range --from 2026-11-09 --to 2026-11-13 --include speech,memos

# Connect an MCP client
$ claude mcp add lore -- /Applications/Lore.app/Contents/Helpers/lore-mcp

Privacy

Your voice stays on your Mac.

Something that listens all day should be clear about where your data goes. Here's exactly where.

On-device speech recognition

Apple SpeechAnalyzer or a whisper.cpp model you download. No cloud speech-to-text.

Audio is never sent

Dictation audio is never saved. Life Log audio stays on your Mac, deleted after transcription by default or kept for 7 or 30 days. Neither is ever sent.

Cloud AI only if you choose it

Formatting with OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or Groq happens only when you select it, and sends only that transcript, your rules, Mode instructions and Vocabulary. Never audio, never your Life Log.

Optional, encrypted Sync

Sync writes AES-GCM-encrypted text to a folder you pick, like iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing. Lore makes no network connection for it, and audio is never synced.
