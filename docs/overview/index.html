macOS voice app

Lore Dictation and a life log that stay on your Mac

Lore types what you say into any app and keeps your speech, memos, and calendar events on one timeline per day. Speech recognition runs on the Mac. Summaries and to-do lists are left to the AI you choose, through the CLI or MCP.

Features

Modes per app

Lore picks a mode from the frontmost app when recording starts and formats the text with instructions that fit the use.

Common rules and vocabulary

Rules for every mode and your own terms are managed separately from the mode instructions.

Traceable history

Each dictation keeps the raw text, the result, the mode, the instructions, the engines, and the timings.

Day timeline

Speech, dictations, memos, and events in time order, with search, a monthly calendar, and a floating mini window.

Voice memos

A hotkey or a button turns a spoken note into a memo on today's timeline.

CLI and MCP

Read-only access to days, ranges, search, and Markdown export for the AI client you choose.

Sync between Macs

Optional. Encrypted change files in a folder you choose carry text data between your Macs.

Privacy boundary

Dictation audio

Held only in memory while it is processed. Never stored and never sent.

Life Log audio

Stored on the Mac only after consent, for recovery, and deleted after transcription by default. Never sent.

On-device formatting

The default. Transcripts and instructions do not leave the Mac.

Cloud formatting

Only when you select it: the current transcript, the common rules, the mode instructions, and the vocabulary go to the selected provider.

Life Log records

Lore never sends them to an LLM or the cloud. You decide which AI client reads them.
