- With Life Log AI on, Lore writes a daily digest at the end of each recording day: a few lines about the day, what you took on, what you asked others for, and what you decided. - A notification with the day's talk time, events, and memos says the digest is ready (it can be turned off), and the next morning's first open shows yesterday's digest once. A day missed while the Mac slept is caught up for yesterday only. (#7)
